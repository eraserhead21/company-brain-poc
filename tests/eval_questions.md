# Cost and quality test: 10 questions

Ask each question twice, in two fresh Claude Code sessions in this repo:

- **Run A, brief first:** "Answer from brief.md and learnings/approved/ only."
- **Run B, source first:** "Answer from the Drive folder and GitHub only. Do not
  read brief.md or learnings/."

After each run, open `/usage` and record the usage share and the answer.
Score answers against `tests/answers/eval_answers.md` (local only).

Pass: run A uses at least 50% less than run B, and run A gets at least 9 of 10
right.

1. What is the current go-live date, and why did it change?
2. Which message broker did the team choose, and why?
3. How does the pipeline handle readings that meters resend?
4. What happens to readings around the daylight-saving change?
5. How long will the 18-month backfill take, and what limits it?
6. What must be true before go-live is allowed?
7. What is the raw-readings retention period?
8. What is the biggest open external risk?
9. What should the next project do earlier, based on this one?
10. Who owns the on-call rota from October?
