PROJECT ALPHA - NEW ELECTRICITY BILL REVIEW

14 November 2026. Present: PM, Tech Lead, Customer Operations lead.

What happened

- On 3 November, 200 households that moved early to the new billing system got their first electricity bill from it.
- The electricity bill looked different: a half-hourly usage chart, a new "read date" line, and the total moved to page 2.
- In the week after, support calls from those households were 4 times the usual level.
- Most callers asked one question: "Is my electricity bill wrong?" Every bill the team checked was correct.

What we learned

- A correct bill that looks different still feels wrong to the customer.
- Before the first bill from a new system, send customers a one-page note: what changed on the bill, and why.

Actions

- Customer Operations drafts the "What's changed on your bill" note before go-live on 28 November.
