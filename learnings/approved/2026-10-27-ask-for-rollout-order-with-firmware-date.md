**Context:** Firmware v2.4 rollout to the meter groups before the shadow run.
**What happened:** The rollout finished on the first 3 meter groups on 27 October. The vendor sent the rollout order for the other groups 5 days after we asked.
**What we learned:** Ask for the meter-group rollout order together with the firmware date, so the shadow-run groups are planned in one step.
