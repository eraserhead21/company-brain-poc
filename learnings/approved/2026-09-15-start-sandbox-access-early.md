**Context:** The project depends on customer sandbox access.
**What happened:** On a previous vendor project, sandbox access took 3 weeks. At kickoff, the TL asked the customer to start the request that day, not after design sign-off.
**What we learned:** Start customer access requests at kickoff. Do not wait for design sign-off.
