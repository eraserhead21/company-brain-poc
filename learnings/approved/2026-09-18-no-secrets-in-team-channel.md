**Context:** The team channel is the shared chat log for the project.
**What happened:** On 18 Sep an Engineer pasted a customer API test token into the channel. The TL asked the team not to paste tokens there. Onboarding notes give the same rule.
**What we learned:** Never paste a secret, token or password into the team channel.
