**Context:** Weekly meeting of 10 November 2026, review of the shadow-run dry run.
**What happened:** The dry run missed 2 meter groups, because the group list for the comparison was copied by hand.
**What we learned:** Generate the meter-group list for the shadow run from the firmware rollout export, not by hand.
