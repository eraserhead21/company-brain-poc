**Context:** The customer's firmware v2.4 rollout depended on the customer's release calendar.
**What happened:** The rollout slipped one week, to 27 October, because it collided with the customer's change-freeze week. The team had never asked for that calendar.
**What we learned:** Ask for the customer's change-freeze calendar at kickoff and plan the dates around it.
