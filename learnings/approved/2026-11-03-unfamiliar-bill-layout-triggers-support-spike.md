**Context:** 200 early-migrated households received their first bill from the new billing system, which changed the layout: a half-hourly usage chart, a new read date line, and the total moved to page 2.

**What happened:** Support calls from these households ran at 4 times the usual level in the following week, mostly asking whether the bill was wrong. The team checked the bills and every one was correct.

**What we learned:** A correct bill that looks different still feels wrong to customers. Send a one-page note explaining what changed and why before the first bill from any new system.
