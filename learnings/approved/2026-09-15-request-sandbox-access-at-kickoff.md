**Context:** The project needs access to the customer's sandbox.
**What happened:** On a previous vendor project, sandbox access took 3 weeks to arrive. At kickoff, the Tech Lead asked the customer contact to start the request that day, not after design sign-off.
**What we learned:** Start the sandbox access request at kickoff. Access can take 3 weeks.
