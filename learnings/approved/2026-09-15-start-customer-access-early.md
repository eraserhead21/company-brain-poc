**Context:** The integration work needs customer sandbox access.
**What happened:** On a previous vendor project, sandbox access took 3 weeks. At kickoff the TL asked for the request to start that day, not after design sign-off.
**What we learned:** Send customer access requests at kickoff. Lead time is weeks, so do not wait for design sign-off.
