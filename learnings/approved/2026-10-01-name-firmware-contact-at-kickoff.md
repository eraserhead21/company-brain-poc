**Context:** A meter firmware fix was owned by the customer, not by the project team.
**What happened:** The team waited 11 days for the firmware answer, because the request went to the customer's general support inbox.
**What we learned:** Name a firmware contact at kickoff and escalate to that person directly, not to a general inbox.
