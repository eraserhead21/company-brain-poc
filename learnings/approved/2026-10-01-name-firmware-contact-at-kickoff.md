**Context:** The meter firmware fix for malformed timestamps is owned by the customer.
**What happened:** The project waited 11 days for the firmware answer, because the request went to the customer's general support inbox.
**What we learned:** Name a firmware contact at kickoff and escalate to that contact directly, not to general support.
