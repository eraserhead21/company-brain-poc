**Context:** The customer firmware v2.4 rollout was due on 20 October, to fix malformed timestamps.
**What happened:** The rollout slipped one week, to 27 October, because it collided with the customer's change-freeze week. The project had never asked for that calendar. The shadow run start moved from 17 November to 19 November. Go-live stayed on 28 November.
**What we learned:** Ask for the customer's change-freeze calendar at kickoff, and plan the dependent dates around it.
